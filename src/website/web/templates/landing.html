Questions

The things new players ask first.

Do I need any mods?

No. The server runs on vanilla Arma 3 on Altis, so there's nothing to download or keep updated. Own Arma 3 on Steam and you're ready.

Where's the server address?

It's pinned in our Discord, along with the TeamSpeak address.{{if .JoinURL}} While the server is up you can also use the Join the server button on this page.{{end}}

How do I join the Police or EMS?

Sign in with Steam, then apply on the Factions page. Faction command reviews applications and you'll hear back on the website and in Discord. Civilian needs no application.

Will I lose my progress if the server restarts?

No. Your character, money, vehicles and houses are saved in our database, and every bank transfer is recorded as its own transaction.

Someone broke the rules. What do I do?

Open a support ticket and choose Player Report. Staff turn reports into cases, and your ticket tells you when the case is closed.

I've been banned. Can I appeal?

Yes. Sign in and your dashboard shows the ban with a form to appeal it. Staff review it alongside the case.

Do I have to show up on the leaderboards?

No, they're opt-in. Turn it on or off from your dashboard at any time.
